The leftovers are holding the kitchen.

Pick three weapons, pick a mode and scramble. Meals revolted against the diner after last call and you have to stop them.

Play Agent Scramble

Hangry Eggs, Rogue Sausages, Cereal Haunts, Watermelon Brutes, Waffle Stacks, Coffee Creeps, and Hot Sauce Squirts. Most leftovers chase. Squirts spit on Lot Raid. Hold F for the tray you always bring.

Take three of seven weapons. Clear waves.

Modes

Lot Raid

Free-roam the diner block, kitchen deck, and Salsa Cantina. Stomach HP is yours. Next wave starts ten seconds after the last leftover dies. Hot Sauce Squirts shoot here. Hold F to raise your tray.

Tower Kitchen

Hold a diner hallway. The kitchen has 1000 HP. Hit ORDER UP to start leftover waves and boss fights. Buy turrets, landmines, ammo, and Pack-a-Punch (6000 a gun) in the upgrades room. Press T anytime for a full ammo restock (200 score).

Boss fights

Lot Raid and Tower Kitchen. After leftover waves 5, 10, 15 etc... the round becomes a Boss Fight. A Brunch monster walks into the floor with a large health bar.

Next leftover waves will not start until the titan is dead. In Tower Kitchen, hit ORDER UP to start the fight.
