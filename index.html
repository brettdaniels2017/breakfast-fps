Briefing

Diner country after last call.

Hangry Eggs, Rogue Sausages, Cereal Haunts, Watermelon Brutes, Waffle Stacks, and Coffee Creeps have taken the lot. They don’t shoot. They path around furniture, climb stairs, and try to eat you — or the kitchen.

Take exactly three of seven breakfast weapons. Clear waves. Don’t get burnt toast.

Service

Two ways to scramble.

Hunt across the block, or hold the hallway while the kitchen takes the beating.

Lot Raid

Free-roam the diner block, kitchen deck, and Salsa Cantina. Stomach HP is yours. Next wave starts ten seconds after the last leftover dies.

Tower Kitchen

Hold a diner hallway. The kitchen has 1000 HP. Hit ORDER UP to start each wave, then buy Toaster Turrets and plant landmines from the upgrades room.
